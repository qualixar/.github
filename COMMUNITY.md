# Qualixar community guide

Discuss AI Reliability Engineering at https://github.com/orgs/qualixar/discussions.

Use Q&A for usage questions, Ideas for proposed workflows, Polls to prioritize examples, Announcements for maintainer updates, and Show and tell for integrations you have actually tried. General is available for questions spanning several products.

Include the product, version, operating system, what you tried and what happened. Use synthetic examples and redact credentials, private prompts, customer data and identifying logs. A minimal reproducible example is more useful than a long unfiltered transcript.

Bug reports belong in the affected repository's issue tracker. Follow the repository's published security-reporting instructions where available; do not post vulnerabilities or secrets publicly.

Be specific and respectful. Disagreement about a result or design is welcome; personal attacks, spam and repeated promotional links are not. Maintainers may moderate content under GitHub's community policies. No response-time commitment is made.

Share measured results with their environment and limitations. Do not describe a demonstration as a benchmark or a preprint as peer-reviewed work. Participation, downloads and documentation remain open without starring a repository.
